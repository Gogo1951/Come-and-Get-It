# Come & Get It // Notes

> Where Come & Get It departs from the Gogo1951 add-on Style Guide, kept so no review raises it again.

## Exceptions

None.

## Decisions

- The End of Support notice prints at every login, right after the welcome message, and has no toggle, so it shows even when the welcome message is off: every player should learn that Come & Get It now lives on inside Tracking Eye.
- Ships Mists and Mainline TOCs alongside the three current targets, matching Tracking Eye's clients, so players on MoP Classic and Retail who find Come & Get It get pointed to Tracking Eye.
